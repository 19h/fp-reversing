## Evaluation, controls and costs

The generator reconstructs nine additional source-defined cells per retained prefix: C+0xb0, C+0x170, C+0x718, C+0x418, F+0x908, F+0x648, F+0x708, S+0x1e0 and S+0xbc4. Thirty further UNSAT checks leave the inherited controller values arbitrary. The eight unrelated setup cells remain symbolic; zero concrete values are assigned to missing cells. The continuous guest remains stopped before the original undefined load at `0xfffffe000a654ae4`.

The new source obligations cover original producers, exhaustion, container publication, action selection, all-status routing, helper/request state, shared-entry state and four scalar/span compositions. All new stores are proved disjoint from the serialized Z extent. This preserves the prior complete nonzero-span induction without simulating a full span using a single final step.

| --- | --- |
| NOP publication store at `0xfffffe000a660b70` | H identity at the schema request fails; stop before entering the helper. |
| Change action literal low16 at `0xfffffe000a635960` to `0xb7cf` | The third action inequality becomes true; stop before following the changed branch. |
| NOP encoded-tag high16 construction at `0xfffffe000a63670c` | Requested key differs; stop before entering the helper. |

All three controls have satisfiable storage/branch/table premises and a satisfiable violation. They mutate copied source only. Original images, native target and IDBs are unchanged.

Selected exhaustion and request preparation take O(1) work and O(1) additional storage; lookup traversal is not measured or completed. Full preceding nonzero-span serialization remains O(L). The independent packet parser takes O(B) time and O(R) record space for B packet bytes and R parsed headers; the four-level bound is fixed. Source/prefix construction is linear in captured bytes, instruction/query DAGs and retained store reconstruction. SMT search has a 60-second per-query limit and is separate from those linear bounds. No native latency or allocator cost follows from these models.
